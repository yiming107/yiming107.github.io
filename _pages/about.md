Hi! I am Yiming, a senior researcher at <a href="https://dvl.fbk.eu/">Fondazione Bruno Kessler</a>. 
My research focuses on visual perception and reasoning for decision-making and control, with applications in robotic automation, human-AI collaboration, and beyond. Recently, I have been leveraging multimodal foundation models to help embodied agents understand their physical environments and natural-language instructions, tackling what makes this hard in practice: cluttered scenes, subtle visual details, and human instructions that are ambiguous, wrong, or context-dependent. This builds on my background in 3D reconstruction and camera localisation, open-world semantic understanding, multimodal representation learning, video anomaly detection, embodied navigation and manipulation, and human-AI interaction. I am a <a href="https://ellis.eu/">ELLIS</a> member.

I work and live in Trento, a beautiful mountain city in northern Italy, balancing me with nature peace and work stress!
